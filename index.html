Price source. Kalshi settles BTC markets on the CF Benchmarks Real-Time Index (BRTI), averaged over the 60 seconds before each checkpoint. BRTI itself requires a paid license, so this page tracks a stand-in: the median of Coinbase, Kraken, Gemini and Crypto.com mid-prices, streamed tick-by-tick from Coinbase with the cross-exchange offset applied. Expect it to sit within a few dollars of Kalshi's number, not match it to the cent. Bands forecast that 60-second average, not the last trade. Method. Volatility blends four estimators: 24h close-to-close, an EWMA (λ = 0.94) of 15-min returns, Garman–Klass on the last 60 one-minute candles, and Parkinson high/low range. That blend is scaled by an hour-of-day seasonality factor from the last ~3 days. Outcomes use a fat-tailed Student-t (ν = 4) rather than a bell curve, because 15-min crypto returns have heavier tails than normal. Bands are centered on the current price with no directional tilt: in backtests, trend, momentum and mean-reversion signals all called 15-minute direction about 50% of the time, while the volatility bands were well calibrated (best $100 band hit 76% vs 73% predicted; 90% range hit 91%). At 15-minute horizons BTC is mostly noise: the edge is in sizing the range correctly, not in calling direction. The track record shows whether the stated odds hold up. Not financial advice.
